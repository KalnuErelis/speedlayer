# SpeedLayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-22

SpeedLayer speeds up web video playback by detecting quiet parts locally in your browser.

## Data Collection

SpeedLayer does not collect personal data for the developer.

SpeedLayer does not transmit browsing history, video URLs, video metadata, settings, hotkeys, saved-time statistics, or extension usage analytics to the developer.

## Local Data

SpeedLayer stores settings and time-saved statistics in Chrome storage on your device. This data is used to keep your preferences, show saved-time totals, and make the extension work across browser sessions.

Some settings can be manually copied to Chrome sync storage from the options page. Chrome sync storage is provided by the browser and may sync through the user's Google Chrome account according to Chrome's own settings.

## Page Access

SpeedLayer needs access to web pages so it can find video and audio elements, measure loudness, and adjust playback speed. Audio analysis happens locally in the browser.

## Third Parties

SpeedLayer does not sell, rent, or share user data.

SpeedLayer does not use remote analytics, advertising SDKs, or external tracking services.
